Why generic workout plans fail

You have probably tried a workout program from the internet. Week 1 goes well. Week 3 you plateau. Week 6 you quit — not because you lacked motivation, but because the program had no idea what was happening with your body.

Generic plans treat everyone the same. They do not know that you bench pressed 80 kg last Tuesday, that you missed two weeks due to illness, or that your squat progresses twice as fast as your overhead press. A truly good workout plan adapts to you — not the other way around.

The Gainfully difference: Before each workout, the app shows what you lifted last time and exactly what to aim for next. Your AI coach has read every session you have ever logged.
